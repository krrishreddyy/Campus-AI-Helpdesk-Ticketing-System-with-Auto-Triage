How it works

From report to resolution in four steps.

1

Raise

Student submits their issue.

2

Auto-Triage

The system analyzes keywords and category.

3

Smart Routing

The ticket is assigned to the appropriate department.

4

Resolution

Staff updates the ticket until it is resolved.

Student→Ticket→AI Auto-Triage→Department→Staff→Resolution
